Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

IUI is a fertility treatment that involves placing sperm inside a woman's uterus to facilitate fertilization. The goal of IUI is to increase the number of sperm that reach the fallopian tubes.

Reviewed by Dr. Raghav Iyer
Who Might Benefit
Couples with unexplained infertility
Women experiencing cervical mucus issues
Couples using donor sperm

Tracking cycles using bloodwork and ultrasound.
